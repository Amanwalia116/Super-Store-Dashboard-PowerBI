# Super-Store-Dashboard-PowerBI
Interactive Super Store Sales Dashboard built using Power BI to analyze total sales, profit, quantity, and cost price. Includes regional and category-wise analysis, KPI tracking, interactive filters, and data visualizations to identify business trends and support better decision-making.

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Super Store
performance across sales, profit, quantity, cost price, regions, and
product categories.

## 🎯 Key KPIs

-   **Sales:** Overall revenue performance
-   **Profit:** Profitability across the business
-   **Quantity:** Total products sold
-   **Cost Price:** Cost-related performance analysis

## 📈 Important Business Insights

-   **Regional comparison:** Sales and profit are analyzed separately
    across regions, helping identify where revenue and profitability are
    strongest.
-   **Category performance:** The category-wise profit chart helps
    compare the contribution of different product categories.
-   **Sub-category analysis:** The funnel visual highlights sales across
    sub-categories, making it easier to compare product-level
    performance.
-   **Sales vs. profit:** Comparing these measures is important because
    high sales do not always translate into high profit.
-   **Location-based analysis:** The City slicer allows users to filter
    the report and investigate performance for individual cities.
-   **Quantity and cost context:** KPI cards provide additional context
    for understanding sales volume and cost alongside financial
    performance.

## 🎯 Project Objective

To convert Super Store data into clear, interactive business insights
and support better decisions about regional performance, product mix,
and profitability.

## 🛠️ Tools & Features

-   Microsoft Power BI
-   KPI cards and interactive slicers
-   Pie and doughnut charts
-   Funnel chart
-   Pivot-style table
